# Membership evidence and current limits

Read the supplied English manual, PDF spread 9, printed pp.14–15, with a rendered-page inspection. The Physical Requirements section states that comfortable, entertained golfers bring friends and can purchase memberships. It does not specify eligibility thresholds, prices, tier upgrades, benefits or resignation rules.

Printable strings from the supplied golf.exe contain Basic, Silver, Gold and Platinum labels; Member/Resigned statuses; Membership Roster; Rounds played, Handicap and Low round labels. Nearby fragments mention applications, inviting friends, customized carts, extra per-hole fees and home sites. These fragments are evidence for systems and vocabulary, not sufficient evidence for precise tier-benefit mappings or numerical rules. No original executable runtime was observed.

Do not label these records as earned members or use a guessed happiness cutoff to certify retail parity. Membership implementation: supplied executable fragments identify applications and Basic/Silver/Gold/Platinum tiers. Archived Sid notes link joining/upgrading to great shots and finishing attitude, and each join/upgrade to a new invitation. The current rule requires a completed round, one recognized great approach and happiness >=4. Those thresholds and equal eligibility across tiers are provisional, not retail measurements. Applications are accepted/declined in the roster; acceptance invites exactly one new identity. No guessed membership purchase fee, per-tier surcharge, housing allowance, cart benefit or resignation rule has been added.
