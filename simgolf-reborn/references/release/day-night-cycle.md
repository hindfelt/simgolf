# Club day and daily reports

Local implementation, not yet deployed. Ruleset 80 introduces eight minutes of daylight followed by ten seconds of night at 1× simulation speed. Pause, hidden local tabs and modal dialogs stop the local simulation as before; the automatic sunset report is non-modal and does not stop sunrise. Shared courses use their server-driven simulation time. Golf-only competition simulation retains its existing update path.

Daylight lighting follows a sunrise-to-sunset curve. During night, scheduled walk-in, airport, marina and helicopter arrivals wait; existing golfers, staff and vehicles continue their current activities. Clicking the day/time button opens up to 30 saved reports.

Reports close at sunset. The first covers opening through sunset; subsequent reports cover the previous sunset through the next, including overnight transactions. Transactions are assigned once with a saved ledger cursor. Income includes all positive cash receipts. Operating costs include negative transactions other than construction and land purchases. Net cash change includes all three categories. The $50,000 opening balance is not income. Golf and snack spend per arrival uses signed green fees plus snack sales divided by arrivals during the accounting period; transport charges, property sales and prizes are excluded from this metric. It is a period ratio, not tracking each visitor's lifetime spend.

Happiness is the time-weighted mean of active golfers' signed happiness (-10 to +10). Empty periods display “No golfers”; negative green fees and unhappy golfers remain signed. Visitors counts successful admissions, including return visits. Existing saves begin collecting at their saved time rather than inventing earlier visitor or happiness data; their first incomplete daylight report is identified as partial. Save validation checks report structure and financial reconciliation. Previous ruleset-79 shared state migrates to 80; compatible golf-only packages retain their ruleset compatibility.

Verification covers sunset boundaries, ten-second nights, multiple-day steps, financial classification, negative fees and happiness, zero visitors, bounded history, save/reload, legacy migration, overnight arrivals and mobile report/sunrise/history behavior. Broader regression result is recorded after completion.

Regression evidence: the broad 502-test live suite finished with 492 passes and 10 failures (7.8 minutes). Signed-happiness validation and a ruleset-79 hardcoded expectation were corrected; focused reruns for the course-edit save and protocol migration pass. Eight browser failures remain to resolve: visitor fee details, boot retry, boundary editing, environment description, land purchase controls, phone palette, phone tee placement and testing-mode land purchase. This is not a clean release regression. Nine focused daily-cycle checks passed across the focused run and added legacy-night migration check. Production build passed.
