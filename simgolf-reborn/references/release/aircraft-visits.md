# Visiting aircraft

Airstrips now display a light aircraft through approach, runway landing, taxi-in, unloading, parking, boarding, taxi-out and departure. This replaces the permanent static plane. The aircraft uses the runway's rotation and elevation, with its wingspan smaller than the runway width plus shoulders; the apron provides clearance beside the terminal.

Each airstrip admits one aircraft at a time. The first scheduled arrival remains four simulated minutes after initialization, and the next is scheduled eight minutes after departure. Existing saved `nextFlight` values are retained. Flights begin only when the airstrip is connected and the course can admit guests. Landing takes 20 seconds of approach, 12 of runway roll, 10 of taxiing and at least five of unloading. Admission waits for daylight and course availability. If disconnected before unloading, the aircraft taxis out empty. Successful admission uses the existing distinct airport visitor pool and increments the facility's served count once.

The plane stays parked with its propeller stopped until both actual passengers finish their golf/recreation visits and reach their exit. Boarding lasts five seconds, taxi-out ten, and departure fifteen. Occupied airstrips cannot be demolished. No new fee is added; existing airstrip green-fee benefits remain unchanged. Flights are saved simulation state, not render-time triggers. Course-layout exports omit live aircraft visits. Ruleset 81 migrates previous shared state and retains golf-only ruleset compatibility.

Validation: 34 targeted tests pass, covering phase sequence, no early/duplicate admission, waiting for both passengers, night/disconnection, pose continuity, occupied demolition protection, real completed rounds, exact save/reload, browser rendering, airport fee accounting, rotated construction, deterministic command replay and daily reports. The approach screenshot was inspected. This is local work, not a hosted deployment; frequencies and the broader transport economy still need playtesting.
